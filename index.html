The Chemical Blueprint

Monomer: Nucleotide

Built from a sugar, a phosphate group, and a nitrogenous base.

Polymer: DNA & RNA

Long chains of nucleotides that form the double helix structure.

Key Elements: CHONP

Carbon, Hydrogen, Oxygen, Nitrogen, and Phosphorus.

Structure-Function

Weak hydrogen bonds allow the double helix to "unzip" for replication.

Case Study: Sickle Cell Anemia

The Mutation

A single base change in the HBB gene replaces Glutamic acid with Valine.

The Impact

This tiny structural change alters the hemoglobin protein, causing red blood cells to become rigid and sickle-shaped.
